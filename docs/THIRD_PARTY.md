# Bundled assets

- The frontend bundles Manrope, Instrument Serif and IBM Plex Mono through their Fontsource packages. Their license files are included in the respective installed packages; preserve license notices when redistributing those assets.
- PDF reports embed DejaVu Sans. Its original font is copied without modification, and its full redistribution notice is bundled at `backend/claimbreaker/assets/fonts/LICENSE-DejaVu.txt`. Source: [DejaVu project](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/).
- Other dependencies retain their own package licenses. Production and test versions are recorded in the lockfiles.
